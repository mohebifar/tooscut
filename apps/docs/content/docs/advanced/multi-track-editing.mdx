---
title: Multi-Track Editing
description: Work with multiple video and audio tracks, linked clips, and track controls.
---

Unlimited tracks for layering video, audio, text, and shapes. Tracks are created in pairs (video + audio). Linked clips keep video and audio in sync.

## Adding tracks

Click **+ Track** in the top-left corner of the timeline. New video track appears above existing ones, matching audio track appears below. The timeline scrolls to show the new tracks.

![Multi-track editing](../_images/multi-track-editing.png)

## Track controls

| Control  | Effect                                           |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| **Mute** | Hide the video track or silence the audio track  |
| **Lock** | Prevent all modifications to clips on this track |

Locked tracks block move, trim, split, and delete operations.

## Removing tracks

Right-click a track header to delete it. Both tracks in the pair are deleted. Confirmation dialog appears if the track contains clips.

The last track pair cannot be deleted.

## Stacking order

Video tracks stack bottom to top — higher tracks render on top. This determines visual layering in preview and export.

## Linked clips

Importing a video file with audio creates two linked clips: video on a video track, audio on the matching audio track.

### Linked behavior

| Operation  | Behavior                           |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------- |
| **Move**   | Both clips move together           |
| **Split**  | Both clips split at the same point |
| **Delete** | Deleting one deletes the other     |
| **Trim**   | Both clips trim together           |

### Unlinking

Right-click a linked clip and choose **Unlink**. After unlinking, each clip operates independently.

## Moving clips between tracks

Drag a clip up or down. Linked clips move to their respective track types.
